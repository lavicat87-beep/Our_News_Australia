About OurNews Australia

OurNews Australia is a community-driven news platform designed as a trusted alternative to noisy social media feeds. The focus is local information only: town announcements, events, school updates, sports, council activity, business notices, photos, and historical stories.

Every community is organized geographically: Australia -> State -> Region -> Town. Each town has its own dedicated page and local archive to keep important information easy to find.

Vision

The long-term vision is to create a national network of locally focused community news pages, preserving local stories and strengthening community connections.

OurNews aims to provide a digital town square where residents can discover what is happening in their area without the distractions, algorithms, and commercial priorities of major social media platforms.

The platform seeks to support local journalism, encourage community participation, and create a permanent, searchable record of community life across Australia.

Mission Statement

To connect Australian communities by providing a dedicated platform where local people can share local news, celebrate local achievements, and strengthen local connections.

Potential Revenue Sources

  • Local business advertising.
  • Featured community listings.
  • Sponsored local events.
  • Council and community organisation partnerships.
  • Premium business profiles.
  • Donations and memberships.
